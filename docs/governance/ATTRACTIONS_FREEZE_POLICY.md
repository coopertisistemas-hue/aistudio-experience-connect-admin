# Attractions Freeze Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-06-01  
**Status:** Active  
**Trigger:** Quality Gate V1 audit findings (U1-C spot check)

---

## Policy Statement

New natural attraction expansion is **temporarily frozen** until verification standards are strengthened. This policy protects portal trust and prevents inventory bloat from unverified or fictional landmarks.

---

## What Is Frozen

| Frozen | Allowed |
|--------|---------|
| New waterfalls not listed on official tourism maps | Verified tour operators and experience providers |
| New viewpoints / peaks without government or tourism board reference | Adventure sports operators with verifiable contact info |
| New caves / natural formations without published references | Cultural attractions with documented history |
| New valleys / scenic areas without established access trails | Farm tourism with CNPJ or tourism registry |
| Generic "trilha da X" entries without official trail status | Ecotourism programs with confirmed guides |

---

## Criteria for Adding a New Attraction

A new natural attraction may be added **only if** it meets **all** of the following:

1. **Named Reference:** The attraction name appears on at least one of:
   - Official municipal tourism website (Prefeitura de Urubici)
   - Santa Catarina state tourism portal (Encantos do Sul, etc.)
   - Established travel guide (Guia 4 Rodas, TripAdvisor with ≥5 reviews, Google Maps with ≥10 reviews)
   - Academic or environmental registry (ICMBio, Parque Nacional de São Joaquim materials)

2. **Coordinate Verification:** Coordinates must be cross-referenced with:
   - Satellite imagery showing the claimed feature (waterfall, peak, viewpoint)
   - Official trail maps or GPS tracks from reputable sources
   - Must not duplicate coordinates of existing entities within 200m

3. **Access Confirmation:** Must confirm:
   - Public or permitted access exists
   - No private land trespass required
   - Trail or road condition is known

4. **Unique Name:** Name must not conflict with:
   - Famous landmarks elsewhere in Brazil (e.g., "Pedra da Gávea" in Rio)
   - Existing published entities in the portal
   - Generic category terms (e.g., "Cachoeira Bonita" without disambiguation)

5. **Human Review:** Must pass manual review by an operator who verifies the above criteria using independent sources.

## Anchor Attraction Enrichment Priority

Instead of adding new attractions, **prioritize enriching existing anchor attractions**:

| Anchor Attraction | Enrichment Actions |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Cachoeira do Avencal | Add seasonal info, access fees, best times |
| Morro da Igreja | Add weather info, access road conditions, photos |
| Serra do Corvo Branco | Add road status, viewpoints along route |
| Parque Nacional de São Joaquim | Add entrance fees, trail maps, camping info |
| Pedra Furada | Add hiking difficulty, duration, equipment needed |
| Cânion Espraiado | Add tour operator links, safety info |

---

## Exceptions

The freeze may be overridden by explicit approval from the Content Governance operator if:
- The attraction is confirmed by direct site visit with photo/video evidence
- The attraction is referenced in an official government tourism publication
- The attraction is part of a funded tourism development project with public documentation

---

## Review Cycle

This freeze will be reviewed:
- After U1-D completion
- When the portal reaches 650 entities
- When a dedicated attraction verification workflow is implemented
- At minimum, every 14 days

---

## Rationale

The U1-C spot check identified **unverified natural attractions** with no external references, no website, and no phone contact. While descriptions are internally consistent, they cannot be independently verified. Publishing unverified landmarks risks:
- Tourists traveling to non-existent locations
- Legal liability if access is on private land
- Portal reputation damage if content is discovered to be fictional
- Wasted enrichment effort on fictional records

This freeze is **protective, not punitive**. It ensures sustainable growth.
